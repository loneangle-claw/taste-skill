How a commission works

  1. Brief

    Send the story, the product or the menu, and where the images will run. I reply with a short treatment and a quote.

  2. Sourcing

    Flowers come from growers and markets in the days before the shoot. Props come from my collection, or from yours.

  3. Shoot day

    Most commissions take one studio day. Join in person, or follow the tethered frames remotely and approve as we go.

  4. Delivery

    Retouched files sized for print, web and social, with usage terms written in plain language.
