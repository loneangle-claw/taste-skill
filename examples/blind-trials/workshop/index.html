What the morning looks like

You watch one arrangement come together, then build your own with a teacher close by. No experience needed.

  1. 10:00

    Arrive and settle in

    Coffee, an apron, and a vessel you choose from the shelf.

  2. 10:30

    Watch a demonstration

    How to cut and condition stems, then build shape, height and colour.

  3. 11:15

    Make your own

    You work at your own pace. We step in whenever you ask.

  4. 12:45

    Wrap it for the trip home

    We pack your arrangement and send you off with a care card.

Everything is provided

Seasonal flowers, your vessel, tools and coffee are all in the price. The vessel is yours to keep.
